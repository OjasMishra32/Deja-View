Deja View

## Description

Walk into the worlds yourself: https://deja-view.photo

Deja View turns the photos and phone videos you already have into 3D places you can walk back into, in a VR headset, a browser or Roblox. We built it at SwampHacks XII after Shivin's dad, who has Alzheimer's, sat in a room rebuilt from one video of him by the fire.

Under the hood: camera poses solved with COLMAP, Gaussian splats trained with gsplat on UF HiPerGator, one-photo worlds from a generative world model, people lifted out of video into 3D, and a reality check that marks every splat a camera never saw. Gemini labels each room and answers questions out loud. Rooms become life size Roblox maps, and the headset and Roblox share one ping pong ball.

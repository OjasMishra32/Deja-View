+ iMessage

Three steps. No 3D skills.

  1. Send a photo or a video.

    Upload it here from your phone or laptop. A room, a game, a night you want back.

  2. We build the room.

    The parts the camera never saw get filled in, behind you included. Seconds on a warm GPU.

  3. Walk in.

    In a VR headset or the browser. Friends can join the same room from Roblox and leave notes.

Photo

One shot. The whole room.

A world model fills in everything the camera missed, so you can turn around. No photo? Describe a room and it gets built anyway.

  1. Back into a corner and shoot wide. Get the floor and the ceiling in the frame.
  2. Lights on, no zoom, landscape. Hold still for a beat before you tap.
  3. Using a clip? Keep it calm. The sharpest frame is the one that gets used.

Video

The real footage, in the room.

Prop up your phone and hit record. You get the room, and the people in it are the real footage standing there in 3D, sound and all.

  1. Prop the phone. Don't hold it. The room is built from whatever stays still.
  2. Whole person in frame, 1 to 3 m away. Feet included, please.
  3. 10 to 30 seconds, good light. Nobody walking through the shot.

Photo · game clip · prompt

Rent Free. Snap any room.

Point, shoot, move in. A world model fills in everything the camera never saw, so you can actually turn around. Game clips work too: we pull the sharpest, steadiest frame. No photo? Describe a room and it gets built anyway.

  • How to shoot
  • 01Back into a corner and shoot wide. Get floor and ceiling in the frame.
  • 02Lights on, no zoom, landscape. Hold still for a beat before you tap.
  • 03Game clips: keep the camera calm. Slow pans, no menus, no motion blur.

One video

Third Wheel. Film your friend cooking.

Prop up your phone and hit record. The kitchen becomes a room you can walk around, and your friend is the real footage lifted into 3D, sizzle and all. You stand right there. Slightly too close. As is tradition.

  • How to shoot
  • 01Prop the phone. Do not hold it. The room is built from whatever stays still.
  • 02Whole person in frame, 1 to 3 m away. Feet included, please.
  • 0310 to 30 seconds, good light. Nobody walking through the shot.

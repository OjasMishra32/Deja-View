One photo · or a prompt

Photo. One shot, the whole room.

Point, shoot, walk in. A world model fills in everything the camera never saw, so you can actually turn around. No photo? Describe a room and it gets built anyway. A calm clip works too: the sharpest, steadiest frame is the one that gets used.

  • How to shoot
  • 01Back into a corner and shoot wide. Get the floor and the ceiling in the frame.
  • 02Lights on, no zoom, landscape. Hold still for a beat before you tap.
  • 03Clips: keep the camera calm. Slow pans, no menus, no motion blur.

One still-camera video

Video. The real footage, in the room.

Prop up your phone and hit record. The room becomes a space you can walk around, and the people in it are the real footage lifted into 3D, sound and all. You stand right there. Slightly too close.

  • How to shoot
  • 01Prop the phone. Do not hold it. The room is built from whatever stays still.
  • 02Whole person in frame, 1 to 3 m away. Feet included, please.
  • 0310 to 30 seconds, good light. Nobody walking through the shot.
